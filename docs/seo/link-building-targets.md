# Link Building Targets & Strategy

Purpose: Provide a ready list of target sites, suggested anchor texts, and outreach copy to increase referring domains and backlinks for EMI Tools.

## High-priority target domains
- investopedia.com (guides, how-to, educational)  
- thebalance.com (personal finance articles)  
- moneycontrol.com (finance news and resources)  
- economictimes.indiatimes.com (financial & banking guides)  
- bankbazaar.com (loan calculators & comparison pages)  
- policybazaar.com (financial education pages)  
- local finance blogs and personal finance newsletters

## Suggested anchor text rotation
- EMI calculator
- Loan EMI calculator
- Free EMI calculator
- Bank-specific EMI calculator
- EMI Calculator Tool (EMI Tools)

## Outreach ideas
- Offer an embeddable iframe (include the snippet) for resource pages and bank blogs.
- Offer to write a guest post about "How EMI changes with rate and tenure" with data and visuals that link to the tools.
- Provide data-driven articles and quick-turnaround infographics that sites can embed with a link back.

## Embed snippet to share
```html
<iframe src="https://emi-tools-master.vercel.app/tools/emi-calculator" width="600" height="700" style="border:0;" loading="lazy"></iframe>
```

## Metrics to track
- Number of referring domains (goal: 10+ within 3 months)
- Number of backlinks (goal: 20+ within 3 months)
- Diversity of IP addresses and referring domains
